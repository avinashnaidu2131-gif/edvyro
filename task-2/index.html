KPI definitions
Churn rate
Churned customers ÷ customers in the current filter.
Retention rate
1 − churn rate (retained customers ÷ customers).
Monthly revenue at risk
Sum of monthly charges of churned customers, i.e. recurring revenue lost. It is also shown as a share of all monthly charges in the filter. On live data, the same profile (Month-to-Month, 3+ tickets, under 15 months) would be applied to active customers.
Tenure KPIs
Average tenure in months, shown for all customers, churned and retained.

Caution: with only 15 customers, one person moves a rate by about 7 points. Patterns are descriptive, not causal, and contract, tenure and tickets overlap heavily. Currency assumed INR. Source: data/customer_churn_clean.csv (validated in Task 1).
